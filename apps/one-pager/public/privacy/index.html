Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tropical Nomads (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our websites (including tropical-nomads.com) and our festival timetable app TN Timetables / Rave Timetables (the “App”), available on the web and as a mobile application.

1. Who we are

Tropical Nomads is a Brazilian psytrance events crew based in Europe. For privacy questions, contact us via Instagram @tropicalnomads.events or through www.tropical-nomads.com.

2. What this App and site do

The App shows festival timetables, stage filters, favorites, and related event information. The website promotes Tropical Nomads events and partners. You can use the App without creating an account.

3. Information we collect

  • Local device data: preferences such as selected festival, favorites, filters, language, and install-help dismissal flags may be stored on your device (for example in browser local storage or app storage) so the App works offline and remembers your choices.
  • Usage analytics: we may collect anonymous or pseudonymous product analytics (for example screen views, festival selections, and button taps) using PostHog to understand how the App is used and improve it. This may include a generated device identifier, approximate technical details (such as app version or browser type), and event metadata. We do not use analytics to sell personal profiles.
  • Technical logs: standard hosting/CDN logs may include IP address, user agent, and request timestamps for security and reliability.
  • Information you share voluntarily: if you contact us on social media or email, we receive whatever you choose to send.

We do not require login, and we do not intentionally collect sensitive categories such as payment card data, precise GPS location for tracking, contacts, or health data through the App.

4. Why we use information

  • Provide timetable features and offline access
  • Remember your preferences on your device
  • Measure App performance and improve features
  • Secure our services and prevent abuse
  • Respond when you contact us

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK where applicable)

Depending on your location, we rely on legitimate interests (operating and improving a free festival timetable), consent where required for non-essential analytics/cookies, and performance of a service you requested.

6. Sharing

We may share limited data with service providers that help us run the App and websites (for example hosting such as AWS Amplify, and analytics such as PostHog). They process data on our instructions. We do not sell your personal information.

7. International transfers

Providers may process data in the EU, UK, United States, or other countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used by those providers.

8. Retention

Device-stored preferences remain until you clear app/browser storage or uninstall the App. Analytics and logs are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, then deleted or aggregated.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Clear site/app storage or uninstall the App to remove local preferences.
  • Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, and to object or withdraw consent.
  • Contact us using the details above to make a request.

10. Children

The App and websites are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
